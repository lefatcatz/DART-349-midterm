Artist Statement

Having always thought everyone should know even a few signs‚ I loved that as a waitress I got to communicate with Deaf customers like I'd always wanted to‚ but hadn't had the chance to before․

When COVID-19 lockdowns pushed me into my apartment‚ it was my first time being part of an online community․ I spent hours and hours with people in various virtual spaces‚ learning to sign in VR‚ and talking to a huge variety of people‚ all without sound․ It opened my eyes to how powerful and accessible this language could be․

This website is the result of that journey‚ and is designed to lower the barrier to entry for sign language and give anyone who is curious a meaningful‚ approachable place to start․

Welcome

Sign Language Perfected is an educational activist website designed to make basic sign language accessible to everyone.

Through simple visuals, step-by-step learning, and an inviting design, users can begin understanding communication beyond spoken language.
